📱 *DRIVER APP*
_The driver flow makes the promise understandable: “your car will be ready on time.”_

🔹 *Sign in*
*Description:* Simple email OTP entry for a driver.
*Core:* Supabase auth; loads driver profile, vehicles, and authorised site/session data.

🔹 *Home / Plug in*
*Description:* Starting screen, centred on current vehicle & cleanest charging window.
*Core:* Scan/enter charger code; view battery state, 24h carbon strip, price signal & recommended window.

🔹 *Session setup*
*Description:* Guided request for energy needed and return deadline.
*Core:* Set kWh/SoC, deadline & mode; show Cheapest, Greenest, Fastest previews; reject impossible requests.

🔹 *Live session*
*Description:* Real-time view after charger is connected.
*Core:* Show charging state, current kW, delivered vs needed, timeline, Green Score; allow "Charge now" or Stop.

🔹 *Session summary*
*Description:* Clear proof of what the scheduler achieved.
*Core:* Final Green Score, cost, renewable share, avoided CO₂, cost saved, and “verified from meter” status.

🔹 *History / Impact*
*Description:* Personal record of clean charging over time.
*Core:* List past sessions; show cumulative savings, avoided CO₂, avg Green Score, best times/sites.

🔹 *Vehicles & settings*
*Description:* Driver preferences and vehicle capabilities.
*Core:* Manage battery capacity, max charging power, default mode, deadline habits & notifications.

🖥️ *OPERATOR CONSOLE*
_The operator flow makes the system defensible: every optimisation and CO₂ saving can be inspected._

🔸 *Overview*
*Description:* Live control-room view for one charging site.
*Core:* Show connected vehicles, site demand vs 100kW cap, carbon intensity, projected cost, peak reduction & avoided CO₂.

🔸 *Forecast & planning*
*Description:* Explains why CleanGrid chooses certain charging periods.
*Core:* Plot 24h carbon, price, base-load & demand forecasts; mark best windows & source freshness.

🔸 *Schedule & load distribution*
*Description:* The core scheduler proof screen.
*Core:* Show planned charging blocks, grid-cap line, total site draw, deadlines & flattened peak. Allow manual replan.

🔸 *Charger fleet*
*Description:* Operational view of OCPP-connected chargers.
*Core:* Status for every connector: availability, current power, driver/session, progress, last heartbeat & reset control.

🔸 *Sessions & exceptions*
*Description:* Queue of active, pending, and at-risk charging sessions.
*Core:* Inspect driver need, delivered energy, deadline risk; operator can override mode/deadline or stop a session.

🔸 *Grid-flex response*
*Description:* Handles grid requests to temporarily reduce site demand.
*Core:* Show requested cap, flexible capacity, affected sessions, projected reward, accept/decline action & ramp-down plan.

🔸 *Impact reports*
*Description:* Defensible sustainability and financial reporting.
*Core:* Compare actual vs “charge immediately” baseline; report shifted energy, avoided CO₂, cost savings & session audit data.

🔸 *Site settings & data health*
*Description:* Administrative control for the operator.
*Core:* Set site grid connection & default mode; review forecast status, cache freshness, OCPP connectivity & logs.
